Project Overview

This project analyzes Olympic sports performance using Tableau dashboards and data visualization. It explores medal distribution by country, top-performing nations, gender participation, and medals won across different sports. The goal is to identify key trends and provide meaningful insights into Olympic performance through interactive visualizations.

Which countries have won the most Olympic medals?

This section highlights the top-performing countries based on their Olympic medal counts and compares their overall performance.

How does gender participation vary in the Olympics?

This section explores male and female participation in Olympic events and shows the distribution of medals by gender.

Which sports have the highest medal counts?

This section compares medal counts across different sports to identify the events contributing the most medals.

How are Olympic medals distributed?

This section analyzes the distribution of gold, silver, and bronze medals to provide a clearer picture of Olympic achievements.

What insights can we learn from Olympic data?

The analysis summarizes important trends in country performance, sports, and gender participation using interactive Tableau visualizations.
